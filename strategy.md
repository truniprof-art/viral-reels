# Strategy — remon_cats (start 2026-11-23, deadline 2027-02-23)

## Platforms
Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts (added 2026-11-23). TikTok: hands off, per Kolya.

## Goal from Kolya
In 3 months, build a unique account that attracts advertisers. Learn from and get inspired by what goes viral (our posts and the niche), but never repost identical videos.

## What advertisers look at (track weekly in log.md)
- Followers on Instagram + YouTube (micro-creator tier starts ~10k; aim for 10k+ on at least one platform by the end).
- Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach. Aim for 3%+ sustained.
- Average views per reel and watch-through rate (IG avg watch time / skip rate, YouTube avg view duration).
- Consistency and identity: recognisable series look (countdown + counter + explosion), regular posting, brand-safe content.
- Proof of "sponsorable" formats: videos where a product or logo could naturally be the hero.

## Milestones
- Month 1 (to 2026-12-23): find what works. ≥10 distinct mechanisms, all 7 shapes tested, a weekly trend scan, and doubling down on the top 2 formats *through variations*.
- Month 2 (to 2027-01-23): build series and interaction. Recurring named series (e.g. "Machine of the day", "Which side wins?"), viewer-chosen mechanisms or shapes from comments, races of two mechanisms side by side, and hooks that make people comment a guess.
- Month 3 (to 2027-02-23): get advertiser-ready. Brand-slot template (logo/product colours as the objects or the machine), media kit (stats, top reels, audience) in mediakit.md, and a list of brand categories that fit (toys, games, tools, snacks, tech).

## Inspiration loop
- Daily (23:00 run): top posts → write the variation ideas into ideas.md (same hook in a new machine/shape/tank, never a repost).
- Weekly (Monday 23:00 run): trend scan with WebSearch (viral satisfying / simulation / marble-race / "balls multiply" formats on Reels and Shorts). Note 3 ideas in ideas.md with the source, adapt the *mechanic or hook*, never copy footage.
- Daily 15:47 run: build the unique video of the day, preferably from the best idea in ideas.md.
